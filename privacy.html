Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Planbord is a private application used by a single household. The only Google account it accesses is the account of its owner, who installed it.

What data is accessed

  • Google Calendar (read-only): events from the owner's calendars, to show them on the planner.
  • Google Tasks: the owner's task lists, to show them and to add or complete items from the planner.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is only used to display the planner on a tablet in the owner's home.
  • The app runs on a server in the owner's home network. It keeps a short-lived in-memory copy of calendar and task data and an encrypted authorization token. Nothing is stored elsewhere.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Planbord's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
